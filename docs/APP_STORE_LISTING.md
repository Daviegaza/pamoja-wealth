# Pamoja Wealth — App Store Listing

## Google Play Store

### Short Description (80 chars)
Group savings & investment platform for chamas across East Africa.

### Full Description
Pamoja Wealth is the modern platform for chamas, investment clubs, and savings groups across East Africa. Track contributions, manage your group treasury, and grow your wealth together — all from your phone.

**BUILT FOR CHAMAS**
• Real-time contribution tracking with M-Pesa STK Push
• Group treasury with double-entry ledger (like a bank)
• Group chat to keep everyone connected
• Democratic voting for group decisions
• Meeting scheduler with RSVPs and minutes
• Public fundraising links for harambees

**TRUST & SECURITY**
• Cryptographically sealed audit trail — every transaction is verifiable
• Multi-signature payouts — no single person controls the funds
• Bank-grade security with biometric login
• KYC verification for member identity

**SMART GOVERNANCE**
• Natural-language rule compiler — write your chama bylaws in plain English
• Automatic rule enforcement on contributions and payouts
• One-tap audit reports for regulatory compliance

**BUILT FOR EAST AFRICA**
• Full M-Pesa integration (STK Push + Paybill)
• Available in English, Kiswahili, and Français
• Works offline — syncs when you're back online
• Multi-currency support (KES, UGX, TZS, RWF, USD, EUR, GBP)

**FREE TO START**
Free for groups up to 10 members. Affordable plans for larger groups. No hidden fees on contributions — just 0.5% capped platform fee.
